Vaskric Grammar

An overview of the grammatical system

Vaskric grammar is agglutinative, ergative, and strictly regulated. Nouns take a plural suffix but no case marking from the noun itself — case is marked by diacritics on the article and on the pronouns, and by the morphosyntactic diacritic layer on the content words themselves. Verbs are synthetic in three tenses, periphrastic in aspect, and never inflect for person.

This hub links to the detailed grammar pages. Read them in any order, but the diacritics page is the keystone — without understanding the diacritic layer, nothing else in the grammar makes full sense.

Key features at a glance

Feature Vaskric behaviour
Morphological type Agglutinative — affixes attach cleanly to roots
Word order SVO in the Neutral Standard; VSO in the High Register
Alignment Ergative — transitive subjects acute, intransitive subjects grave, transitive objects diaeresis
Verb agreement None — the pronoun carries all person and number information
Tenses Three synthetic: present (unmarked), past (-t), future (-r)
Aspect Periphrastic: progressive es, habitual sol, perfect ha
Mood Indicative, imperative (bare stem), conditional (-ev), subjunctive (-isse)
Voice Active, passive (bir + past participle)
Plural Noun suffix -s; pronouns have singular / dual / plural
Gender None
Adjective agreement None — adjectives are invariant
Negation Prefix ne- on the verb
Questions Yes/no questions with sentence-final ka; wh-questions with wh-word fronted
Registers High, Neutral Standard, Contact

Pronouns at a glance

Pronouns are fusional — each is a single unanalysable word — and there is no gender distinction in the third person. Number is singular / dual / plural.

Subject forms

Singular Dual Plural
1stek — Ivet — we twovis — we
2ndtu — youyut — you twoyus — you all
3rdan — he/she/itzat — they twozas — they

Object (accusative) and possessive (genitive) forms exist, marked by diacritics. See Syntax for the full case system.

The verb at a glance

The verb system is built from a single root plus suffix layers. Here is the complete paradigm for vaskren “to speak” in one place:

Form IPA Meaning
vaskren/vaskren/to speak (infinitive)
vaskra/vaskra/speak(s) (present)
vaskrat/vaskrat/spoke (past)
vaskrar/vaskrar/will speak (future)
ha vaskrat/ha vaskrat/has spoken (perfect)
es vaskra/es vaskra/is speaking (progressive)
sol vaskra/sol vaskra/used to speak (habitual)
vaskrev/vaskrev/would speak (conditional)
vaskrisse/vaskrisse/were to speak (subjunctive)
bir vaskrat/bir vaskrat/is spoken (passive)
vaskra!/vaskra/speak! (imperative)
vasking/vaskiŋ/speaking (gerund)
nevaskra/nevaskra/does not speak

No person agreement. The pronoun carries person and number; the verb carries tense, aspect, and mood only.
