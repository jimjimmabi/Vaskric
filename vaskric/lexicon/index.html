Vaskric Lexicon

Core vocabulary — seed set

The Vaskric lexicon is built on roots that are at least two syllables long. Monosyllables survive only as grammatical particles (pronouns, prepositions, conjunctions, numbers). What follows is the seed set — the vocabulary a learner needs first — organized by semantic field.

For roots and affixation, see the Root Structure section at the bottom of this page. For a full searchable index, use Search.

Root structure

A bare root is at least two syllables. Monosyllables are closed-class only: pronouns, prepositions, conjunctions, numbers, particles.

Affixation

Affixes attach after the root. Compounds join two full roots with a hyphen.

FormMeaningBreakdown

Loanroot exclusion

Sinitic loans such as chā¹, mài², dòu⁴ are excluded from the two-syllable rule because they are loans, not native roots. They are always marked with the macron and tone numeral.
