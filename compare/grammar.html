Grammar Comparison

The same skeleton, different flesh

The most remarkable thing about Vaskoryŏ is that despite sounding completely different to the ear and looking completely different on the page, its grammatical structure is identical to Standard Vaskric. Both dialects share the same Latinate core, the same tense system, and the same morphological rules.

This page puts the two grammars side-by-side to demonstrate the deep structural unity of the Vaskrin language family.

❧ ❧ ❧

II. The Verb Paradigm (vaskren - to speak)

You can see the exact structural parallel here. The only major difference is the future tense suffix (-r in the West becomes -l in the East due to the final r → l sound shift) and the passive auxiliary (bir becomes pir).

Present vaskra vaskra 바스카
Past vaskrat vaskrat 바스캇
Future vaskrar vaskral 바스칼
Perfect ha vaskrat ha vaskrat 하바스캇
Progressive es vaskra es vaskra 에스 바스카
Habitual sol vaskra sol vaskra 솔 바스카
Conditional vaskrev vaskrev 바스크레브
Subjunctive vaskrisse vaskrisse 바스크리쎄
Passive bir vaskrat pir vaskrat 피르 바스캇

III. Pronouns

The pronoun system is preserved entirely intact. The only difference is the phonological adaptation of the consonants.

IV. Syntax Example

A basic sentence showing how the SVO order and prepositions are preserved, despite the complete script change.

English: We walk to the house and see the sun.

Vaskric: Vis vandren to l’domus e videren l’solar.

Vaskoryŏ: 비스 반드렌 토 르도무스 에 비데렌 르솔라.

Notice that in the Vaskoryŏ version, vandren becomes 반드렌 (initial v → b), and domus becomes 도무스. But the word order, the preposition to, and the conjunction e are exactly the same.
