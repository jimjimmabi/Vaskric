Cognates & Sound Shifts

How the same roots evolved differently

The core vocabulary of both dialects comes from the same Latinate roots. But centuries of separation and contact with different languages (Sinitic for Vaskric, Korean for Vaskoryŏ) caused the sounds to shift systematically. If you know the rules, you can often predict how a Vaskric word will sound in Vaskoryŏ.

Here are the key sound changes, followed by a table of common cognates.

The Four Great Shifts (Vaskoryŏ specific)

Rule Standard Vaskric Vaskoryŏ Example
Final voiced stops devoice b, d, g p, t, k libren → 리프렌
Initial r → n r- n- rīren → 니렌
Final r → l -r -l vaskrar → 바스크랄
Complex onsets simplify vaskr- bask- vaskra → 바스카

The lexicon is the strongest link between Vaskoryŏ and Standard Vaskric. Words are adapted to Korean phonology but remain completely recognizable. The grammatical core — SVO order, tense suffixes, negation, vocabulary — remains recognizably Vaskric.
