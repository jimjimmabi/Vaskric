Idioms & Metaphors

How the Vaskrins see the world

Language is not just vocabulary; it is a way of seeing the world. Vaskric idioms use the physical landscape of their environment — mountains, snow, and fire — alongside their historical scars of suppression and isolation, to express deep abstract concepts.

III. Poetic Structures

The Vaskrin culture values poetic expression, and the language’s syntax allows for a specific kind of poetic license.

The Poetic Fronting Rule

Instead of Ek semper te amo (I always love you),
you move semper (always) to the front:
Semper, ek te amo. (Always, I love you.)

Vaskric poets often borrow directly from Latin roots to give their work an archaic, heavy, or sacred feel.
