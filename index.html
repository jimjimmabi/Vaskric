The Divergence

The Vaskrin people were once a single community. The Mongol invasions of the 13th century split them apart. One faction fled south and west, becoming the Vaskric people. The other fled east to the Korean peninsula, becoming the Vaskoryō. This is the story of how a single Latin core fractured into two distinct dialects.
