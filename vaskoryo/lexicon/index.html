Vaskoryŏ Lexicon

The Latinate core, in Hangul, with the eastern consonant shifts

The Vaskoryŏ lexicon is the same as Vaskric’s in its roots — the Latinate vocabulary that both dialects inherited from the Roman settlers. What differs is the surface form: the eastern consonant shifts, applied systematically, plus the Hangul script that renders the whole thing.

The columns below show the Latin script, the Hangul form, the IPA, and the meaning. Compare with the Vaskric lexicon to see the shifts in action. For a searchable view, use Search.
