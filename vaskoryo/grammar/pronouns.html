Pronouns

Person, number, and the dual system in the Western Dialect

Vaskric pronouns are fusional, meaning they pack a lot of information into a single word. They distinguish between singular, dual (exactly two), and plural. Because verb conjugation does not encode person or number, the pronoun is always required to understand who is doing the action.

II. Ergative Alignment and Pronouns

Because Vaskric is an ergative language, the pronoun changes form depending on whether it is the subject of a transitive verb (doing an action to something else) or an intransitive verb (just existing or moving).

  • Intransitive Subject: eko vandren — I walk (marked with grave accent).
  • Transitive Subject: eeko vaskra — I speak (something) (marked with acute accent).
